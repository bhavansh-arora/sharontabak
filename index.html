Hello, I’m Sharon

I’m a certified Jay Shetty Life Coach, and I’ve been where you are. I’ve walked through the loneliness, the anxiety, the pain of broken relationships, and the exhaustion of trying to keep it all together. But I’ve also found a way through—and now I’m here to help you do the same.

Over the last four years, I’ve supported women navigating emotional abuse, toxic marriages, gender identity struggles, mental health crises, and the silent battles no one else sees. Together, we create a path forward—one grounded in peace, strength, and emotional freedom.

How transformation unfolds

From cocoon to wings.

Coaching is like a caterpillar becoming a butterfly: shedding old beliefs, gaining strength, and emerging free, confident, and aligned with your purpose.

01 — The cocoon

A safe place to land

We begin by creating a safe, supportive space, your cocoon, where you can unpack your thoughts, feelings and goals without judgment.

02 — Shedding

Letting go of what holds you back

Together we spot the beliefs and mental blocks that keep you stuck, and get clear on what truly matters to you.

03 — Strength

Your strengths come alive

New perspectives take root. You take intentional steps, not perfectly, but with purpose, and feel lighter after every session.

04 — Emergence

Ready to soar

You emerge empowered, free, and ready to build a life aligned with your purpose, filled with clarity, confidence and peace.
