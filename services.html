What coaching is

Coaching isn’t about me telling you what to do or giving you a list of answers. It’s about creating a safe, supportive space where you can unpack your thoughts, feelings, and goals.

My role is to guide you in spotting what might be holding you back, getting clear on what really matters to you, and helping you take intentional steps toward the life you want.

Think of it like holding up a mirror—you get to see yourself more clearly and make choices that truly align with your purpose.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Still wondering? Ask me anything on your free call, or send a message.

What happens on the free call?

It’s a free, 30-minute conversation—no pressure, no cost. We’ll talk about where you are, what you’d love to change, and whether we’re the right fit to work together. You’re welcome to just listen and ask questions.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is about creating a safe, supportive space for self-discovery—not giving advice, diagnosing or treating. It’s focused on clarity and forward movement, and it can sit alongside therapy or medical care, but it doesn’t replace them.

Who is coaching for?

This space is for men and women from all walks of life, aged 18 to 100, who want to feel less alone and more alive—whether you’re healing from trauma or a toxic relationship, questioning your identity, or navigating a big life transition.

Which package should I choose?

A single session is perfect for focused support on one challenge, or to experience coaching before committing. The 3-month package (13 sessions) builds clarity and momentum. The 6-month package (26 sessions) is for bigger shifts and lasting transformation. We can decide together on your free call.

Can I try one session before committing?

Yes. The one-time session ($250 per hour) exists exactly for that, so you can feel what coaching with me is like before choosing a longer journey.

What will sessions feel like?

Soulful, not superficial. We talk honestly and openly. I listen deeply, help you find your strengths and overcome mental blocks, and we move forward—not perfectly, but with intention. You’ll leave every session feeling lighter, more understood, and more in control of your life.

Coaching is not a substitute for therapy, medical or crisis care. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please call 911, or call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) any time.
